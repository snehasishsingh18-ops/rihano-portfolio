What I can help with

Services

Flexible audio services for artists, creators and brands. Scope, turnaround and deliverables are agreed before every project.

01 / PRODUCTION

Music & Beat Production

Build a track around your sound, mood and creative direction.

  • Hip-hop, trap and rap beats
  • Dark, aggressive and cinematic instrumentals
  • Drums, 808s and arrangement
  • Beat customization and demos
02 / VOCALS

Vocal Editing & Prep

Organize and polish vocal recordings before the final mix.

  • Vocal cleanup and comping
  • Timing edits and take selection
  • Ad-lib and vocal-layer organization
  • Mix-ready vocal files
03 / MIXING

Mixing Services

Bring vocals and instrumentals together with focus and balance.

  • Rap vocal and beat balancing
  • EQ, dynamics and effects
  • Vocal clarity and impact
  • Mix revisions by agreement
04 / FINAL AUDIO

Mastering

Final-level polish and delivery for projects ready for mastering.

  • Stereo mastering
  • Tonal and peak checks
  • WAV / MP3 delivery
  • Alternate versions by request
05 / CREATORS

Audio for Content

Audio support for creators, video editors and small teams.

  • Reels, Shorts and YouTube audio edits
  • Voice-over cleanup
  • Music edits and transitions
  • Podcast cleanup (project-dependent)
06 / SOUND

Sound Design & Branding

Give content a more distinctive sonic identity.

  • Producer tags and audio logos
  • Short-form sound effects
  • Impacts, risers and transitions
  • Atmospheric and cinematic textures
Clear scope, honest delivery. Services are confirmed per project. Advanced mastering, complex restoration, film post-production and other specialist work are offered only where the project requirements and available setup are a good fit.
